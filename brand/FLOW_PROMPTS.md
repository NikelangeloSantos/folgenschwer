# FOLGENSCHWER – Prompts für Google Flow, Version 4: hell, modern, für Jugendliche & junge Erwachsene

Stil: hell und knallig, aber cool statt niedlich. Kräftige Farbverläufe, saubere 3D-Formen, Bewegung und Energie, wie ein modernes Poster oder Gaming-/Tech-Branding.
Farben: Electric Blue #2F6BFF · Violett #7B3BFF · Neon-Orange #FF6A1A · Weiß · Akzent Cyan #3FE0FF.
Symbole: Fragezeichen + Dominos/Kette + Bruch/Energie.

## Logo 1 – 3D-Fragezeichen mit Domino-Punkt (Empfehlung)
Bold modern logo icon: a sleek glossy 3D question mark in vivid neon orange (#FF6A1A) with sharp white highlights, the dot of the question mark is a single tilted domino tile mid-fall with a burst of small light particles, vibrant gradient background from electric blue (#2F6BFF) to violet (#7B3BFF), subtle cyan (#3FE0FF) rim light, clean premium 3D render, high energy, punchy contrast, readable at tiny sizes, centered with padding for a circle crop, no text, 1:1 square

## Logo 2 – „F“ mit Energie-Riss (helle Version deines jetzigen Logos)
Bold modern monogram logo: a thick geometric capital letter F in glossy white with soft chrome edges, split diagonally by a bright neon orange (#FF6A1A) energy crack with a few sharp sparks flying out, vibrant gradient background from electric blue (#2F6BFF) to violet (#7B3BFF), cyan (#3FE0FF) rim light, clean premium 3D render, streetwear and tech brand vibe, high contrast, readable as a tiny app icon, centered with padding for a circle crop, 1:1 square

## Banner 1 – Domino-Kettenreaktion (Empfehlung)
Bright dynamic ultra-wide banner, 16:9, vibrant gradient background flowing from electric blue (#2F6BFF) on the left to violet (#7B3BFF) on the right with soft light streaks, a long line of sleek glossy dominoes sweeping across the full width in a dynamic curve and tipping over one after another, each domino shows a bold modern icon: a smartphone, a game controller, a coin, an AI chip, a brain, a rocket, a planet, a lightning bolt, the dominoes glow with neon orange (#FF6A1A) edges, small bursts of light where they hit, the chain ends on the right in a large glossy neon orange 3D question mark, clean premium 3D render, motion and energy, the central horizontal band kept calm and slightly darker for a title, no text, no letters

## Banner 2 – Zerbrechende Kette in Hell
Bright dynamic 16:9 banner, vibrant electric blue (#2F6BFF) to violet (#7B3BFF) gradient sky with soft light rays, a sleek glossy white chain stretching across the frame, in the center one link snaps apart in a bright neon orange (#FF6A1A) energy burst with a clean ring shockwave and flying sparks, around it floating bold modern icons in white outline style: a smartphone, a stock chart, a DNA helix, a satellite, a clock, a game controller, cyan (#3FE0FF) accents, premium 3D render, high energy, not dark, calm area in the middle band for a title, no text, no letters
