# FOLGENSCHWER – Prompts für Google Flow, Version 3: hell & kinderfreundlich

Stil: heller, verspielter 3D-Cartoon-Look, weiche runde Formen, fröhliche Farben, trotzdem klar und plakativ.
Farben: Sonnen-Orange #FF7A2F · Himmelblau #4FC3F7 · Sonnengelb #FFD23F · Minzgrün #5EE6B0 · Weiß.
Symbole bleiben: Fragezeichen (Was wäre, wenn …?) + Dominos/Kette (eins führt zum anderen).

## Logo 1 – Fröhliches Domino-Fragezeichen (Empfehlung)
Cute 3D cartoon logo icon: a big friendly glossy orange (#FF7A2F) question mark made of chunky rounded dominoes, the dot of the question mark is a bouncing yellow (#FFD23F) ball with little sparkles and confetti around it, soft clay-like 3D render with rounded edges, bright sky-blue (#4FC3F7) circular background with a soft white gradient glow, playful and kid-friendly, clean bold silhouette readable at tiny sizes, soft shadows, centered with padding for a circle crop, no text, 1:1 square

## Logo 2 – Freundliches „F“
Playful 3D cartoon logo, a chunky rounded capital letter F in glossy bright orange (#FF7A2F) with a white highlight, a small row of colorful dominoes tipping over against it, little yellow stars and confetti popping out, bright sky-blue (#4FC3F7) background with soft clouds, cheerful, kid-friendly, soft shadows, clean simple shapes readable as a small app icon, centered with padding for a circle crop, 1:1 square

## Logo 3 – Maskottchen (falls ihr ein Gesicht für den Kanal wollt)
Cute 3D cartoon mascot logo: a round friendly orange (#FF7A2F) domino character with big curious eyes and a happy open smile, holding a giant yellow (#FFD23F) question mark, tiny sparkles around, bright sky-blue (#4FC3F7) background, soft clay-like render, kid-friendly, bold readable silhouette, centered with padding for a circle crop, no text, 1:1 square

## Banner (YouTube, 16:9)
Bright cheerful ultra-wide banner, 16:9, sunny sky-blue (#4FC3F7) gradient background with soft white fluffy clouds and floating confetti, a long playful line of colorful chunky 3D dominoes (orange #FF7A2F, yellow #FFD23F, mint #5EE6B0, sky-blue) curving across the whole width and tipping over one after another, each domino shows a cute simple icon: a rocket, a smartphone, a coin, a light bulb, a little planet, a friendly dinosaur, a clock, a brain, the chain reaction ends on the right in a big glossy orange question mark surrounded by sparkles, soft clay-like 3D cartoon render, soft shadows, joyful and kid-friendly, the central horizontal band kept clean and calm (light sky) for a title, no text, no letters

## Banner-Variante – Kettenreaktions-Maschine
Bright playful 16:9 banner, a colorful cartoon chain-reaction machine stretching across the frame: dominoes knock over a ball that rolls down a ramp, hits a bell, launches a tiny paper rocket and lights up a big glowing question mark, cheerful colors orange (#FF7A2F), yellow (#FFD23F), mint (#5EE6B0), sky-blue (#4FC3F7), white clouds and confetti in a light sky background, soft 3D clay-like render, kid-friendly, clean empty light band in the center for a title, no text, no letters

## Tipps
- Immer „no text“. Den Schriftzug FOLGENSCHWER setze ich danach drauf (dunkles Navy mit weißem Rand, damit er auf hellem Grund knallt).
- Pro Prompt 4 Varianten erzeugen und die besten 1–2 schicken.
